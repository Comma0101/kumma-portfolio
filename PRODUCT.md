# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People finding Kumma Wu and encountering his perspective, with direct access to his writing and work. The precise primary audience and its priorities remain open; do not assume recruiters, clients, or a particular philosophical community.

## Product Purpose

A personal site centred on Kumma Wu’s inquiry into consciousness and self-awareness. Consciousness concerns the nature of experience, the self, and their relation to reality; self-awareness concerns noticing thoughts, emotions, habits, and choices. Both are explicitly important to him. The site should communicate his perspective and how he lives, with software, writing, and reading as parts of that life. His confirmed interests also include training, trading, philosophy, religion, and history.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing site uses Next.js 14, React, TypeScript, and npm, with static export for GitHub Pages. Work write-ups live under `/work`; writing lives under `/notes`, including existing Chinese editions. `/studies` contains browser-rendered website artwork. Preserve working destinations, static deployment, and unrelated local changes.

The rich eight-chapter experience at `/` is the primary replacement site. It retains continuous ink, expressive variable type, Chinese typography, brushwork, and a developed personal narrative, with refined wording around consciousness and self-awareness. Work, Writing, and Email are directly reachable from the opening, with contact paths available again at the end. Project showcases belong under Work. The user has authorized the full replacement and publication after preparation and release checks; deployment success must be verified separately.

The compact `/view` experiment remains available for comparison: a spiral portrait, an introduction to awareness and consciousness, one quiet dated excerpt from existing personal writing, and paths to Writing, Work, X, and email. It remains noindex and is not the intended replacement for `/`.

The user is learning to draw and explicitly excludes “draw” as a personal identity or established practice. Website artwork is design craft, not evidence of artistic expertise.

## Brand Commitments

Public identity: Kumma Wu, also Kumma. Use direct, personal language with conviction and room for uncertainty. Lead with his inquiry into awareness and consciousness rather than his profession. Visual animation and typography are the site's primary expressive language, not a finishing layer over prose. The user values meaningful art, typography, and motion. Generic aphorisms, a quote anthology, and a tidy invented biography have been rejected. The central interests are confirmed; a metaphysical theory, religious affiliation, or spiritual attainment is not. Do not invent beliefs, life events, credentials, expertise, private information, or proof.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing software write-ups, essays, public identity links, the procedural spiral portrait, and browser-rendered linework. Use source content and confirmed statements; distinguish exploratory ideas from completed work.

## Product Principles

- Let actual work and writing substantiate the person’s perspective; preserve their independent purposes.
- Keep the main introduction centred on the person and his inquiry; offer projects through Work rather than a featured case study in the main journey.
- Keep comparisons inspectable and uncertainty honest.
- Preserve the rich `/` experience while grounding its language in confirmed interests, particular writing, and ongoing questions.
- Preserve access through keyboard, touch, and reduced motion.
- Keep experiments scoped to the route under review.

## Open Decisions

The primary `/` structure and intent to publish its replacement are confirmed. Kumma’s account of his worldview can continue to develop through specific writing; this does not imply a settled metaphysical theory. Audience precision and any future change of visual identity remain open.
