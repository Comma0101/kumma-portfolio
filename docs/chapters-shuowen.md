# The eight chapters and the Shuowen Jiezi

The homepage's chapter readings (components/personal/chapters.ts) follow
the Shuowen Jiezi 說文解字 (Xu Shen, c. 100 CE). Source lines:

| Chapter | Shuowen | Reading on the page |
|---|---|---|
| 名 Name | 名，自命也。从口从夕。夕者，冥也。冥不相見，故以口自名。 | Dusk and a mouth: in the dark, where no one can see you, you call out your own name. |
| 主 Agency | 主，鐙中火主也。 | A lamp, and the flame that stands in it. |
| 知 Understanding | 知，詞也。从口从矢。 (Duan Yucai: 識敏，故出於口者疾如矢也) | An arrow and a mouth: knowing comes quick as an arrow. The body is slower. |
| 步 Steps | 步，行也。从止𣥂相背。 | Two feet, one ahead of the other. |
| 習 Practice | 習，數飛也。从羽从白。 | Wings: a young bird flying, again and again. |
| 律 Rules | 律，均布也。从彳聿聲。 | The road, and a hand holding a brush: to keep things even. (聿 is the phonetic in the Shuowen; reading it also as the brush is a gloss on the form.) |
| 理 Reason | 理，治玉也。从玉里聲。 | Jade, worked along its grain. |
| 道 The way | 道，所行道也。从辵从首。 | A head, walking: the road you walk. |

"The body is slower" is the page's addition, tying 知 to its chapter
("Understanding arrives first. The body catches up.").
