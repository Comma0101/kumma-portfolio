# SMB Outreach Playbook (the deal engine)

From a 2026-06-17 research sweep on how an independent AI engineer lands first SMB
clients for custom AI-agent work. Companion to the `/build` landing page. The page
is the brochure; THIS is what gets the deal.

## The core truth

Non-technical SMB owners do not browse for "custom AI agents." First deals come
from **vertical cold outreach + a callable demo + referral/white-label
partnerships**, not from being found. Documented solo benchmark: 83 targeted
emails -> 14 replies -> 7 calls -> 5 clients ($6.4K) in 28 days. Economics are
strong: $1.5-5K single-agent builds (up to $8-15K for med spa systems), $300-800/mo
to run a single agent, $1-3K/mo care retainers, 60-80% margins.

## Beachhead verticals

- **Primary: med spas / aesthetic clinics.** High willingness to pay ($8-15K setup
  + ~$1,500/mo common), booking/intake/reminders/follow-up map directly to agent
  work, non-technical owners, growing market.
- **Secondary: home services (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing).** High call
  volume, owners feel every missed after-hours call ($275-$1,200 each), phone-driven.
- **Proof only, not a target: restaurants.** KOTA lives here, but it is the hardest
  to monetize (crowded, thin margins). Use KOTA as the demo story; sell elsewhere.
- Rule: saturated at the generic level, wide open at the niche level. Pick ONE
  vertical, dominate it, then expand.

## Offer & funnel

- One offer to lead with: "AI front desk that answers every call, books
  appointments, and follows up, so you stop losing $X/mo in missed calls."
- **Free = the consult/audit only** (15-20 min: quantify their leak, show the
  demo). **Never free = the build.** Bridge with a **paid pilot** (scoped to one
  use case + one metric, reduced setup fee) and a "keep it or we remove it"
  guarantee. Paid pilots filter tire-kickers and anchor the real price.
- Cap scope in writing: pilot covers one workflow for one location; other
  workflows quoted separately.

## Channels, ranked by speed-to-first-deal

1. **Vertical cold outreach** (email + call + DM) with an ROI hook. Fastest.
   Targeted/local reply rates run well above the ~3.4% generic average.
2. **Referral / white-label partnerships** (web designers, marketing agencies,
   POS/booking vendors, MSPs, accountants). They own the SMB relationship; you are
   the build skill. Rev-share or white-label.
3. **Niche owner communities** (vertical Facebook groups, subreddits, trade assns,
   BNI). Be helpful, do not pitch.
4. **Marketplaces** (Upwork, Fiverr Pro, Clutch) for inbound while building proof.
5. In-person/local networking (referral seed).
6. Local SEO + Google Business (compounds, slow).
7. Paid ads — not first; no proof or offer-market fit yet.

## 30-day action plan (goal: 5-10 consults, 1 paid deal)

- **Week 1 — positioning + proof + list.** Lock one vertical + the missed-call
  offer. Build a 60-second KOTA call recording and (ideally) a callable demo
  number. Build a 150-prospect local list (Google Maps + sites; note owner name,
  booking method, whether calls hit voicemail). Write the one-page offer + pilot terms.
- **Week 2 — outreach launch.** 25-30 personalized emails/day + 10-15 calls/day +
  owner DMs. Post helpfully in 2-3 vertical communities. Target ~120 emails, ~60
  calls, 2-4 consults booked.
- **Week 3 — consults + partnerships.** Run free missed-revenue audits, pitch the
  paid pilot. DM 15-20 local web/marketing agencies + booking vendors with a
  white-label/rev-share offer. Follow up all non-responders (touch 3-4).
- **Week 4 — close + systematize.** Close 1 paid pilot, deliver fast, capture a
  recorded demo + testimonial, re-touch warm prospects with it. Refill pipeline.

## Outreach assets (templates)

**Cold email (med spa):**
> Subject: missed calls at [Spa Name]?
>
> Hi [First name] — I called [Spa Name] yesterday after hours and reached
> voicemail. Missed-call rates for clinics run around 60%, and most of those
> callers just book with a competitor.
>
> I build AI front-desk agents that answer every call 24/7, book appointments into
> your system, and text back missed leads. I run one live for a restaurant in LA;
> you can call it and hear it: [demo]. Worth a 15-min look at how many bookings
> [Spa Name] is leaving on the table? I will do the math on a quick call, free.
>
> — Kumma, AI engineer, LA

**Cold call opener (home services):**
> "Quick one — when you are on a job and a call comes in, where does it go?
> ... Right. I build an AI receptionist that picks up every one of those, books the
> appointment, and texts you the lead. Costs less than the jobs you are missing.
> Can I send you a number to call so you hear it yourself?"

**Partner DM (web/marketing agency):**
> "You build sites for [vertical] clients. I build the AI phone/booking agents that
> sit on top. Want to offer it under your brand? You keep the client and a margin,
> I handle the tech. Happy to white-label or rev-share."

## Objection handling (quick reference)

- Too expensive -> frame against the cost of the problem (missed calls = lost
  bookings); cheaper than a part-time hire, never misses a call.
- Not for a business like mine -> hyper-specific vertical use case + callable demo.
- Will it actually work / embarrass me -> live demo + guaranteed human handoff.
- Can I trust a solo guy -> named case study with a number, demo, money-back pilot,
  local presence, fast replies, open-source ARCHON as proof.
- My data / privacy -> "your data is never used to train AI models; it stays yours."
- Who maintains it -> a priced monthly care plan, stated up front.

## Numbers worth quoting (sourced in the research)

- ~37.8% of SMB calls answered live; ~62% unanswered; ~62% of those call a
  competitor; ~85% never call back.
- Cost per missed call: dental ~$850, home services $275-$1,200, law $5,000+.
- AI receptionist: ~300% first-year ROI; $25-$569/mo vs a $2,800-$3,500/mo human.

## Highest-value thing to build next

A **public callable demo number** for KOTA. "Call this and hear it work" is the
single strongest converter for this audience and powers every outreach channel above.
